Sealed-bid auction

Bidders publish a cryptographic commitment to their bid while keeping its amount and salt private. After bidding closes, bidders reveal their receipts; revealed amounts and the current winner then become public on-chain.

Commit a bid

The amount and random salt are saved only in a receipt downloaded to your device. Keep it private and backed up: you need it to reveal. The chain receives only the commitment hash in this phase.

Close bidding

Only the creator can close the global commit phase. Once closed, new bid commitments are rejected.

Reveal a bid

Revealing makes your bid amount public. Reveals can be submitted by anyone who has the private receipt.

Finalize

Finalize after the desired bids have been revealed. The contract records the highest revealed bid meeting the reserve.
